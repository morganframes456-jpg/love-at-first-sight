Chapter Four

The Memory That Remained

✦ ───── ♡ ───── ✦
KISUBI HIGH SCHOOL • THE FINAL DAYS
Some people leave our lives, but the memories they gave us remain long after they are gone.

After the dance evening, Kevin and Diana continued to avoid each other.

Days passed, but the silence between them remained. The friendship that had once felt so natural had suddenly become difficult.

Kevin sometimes saw Diana walking through the school compound, but he no longer knew whether he should approach her.

Diana seemed to feel the same way.

They were both carrying memories of everything that had happened, yet neither knew how to return to the friendship they once had.

During all of this, Kevin had something he kept private.

He had a notebook.

Inside that notebook, he wrote about different experiences from his life. But among its pages were memories about Diana.

He wrote about the first time they met.

He wrote about the accidental bump that started everything.

He wrote about their conversations, the basketball pitch, their friendship and the dance evening.

To Kevin, the notebook was simply a place where he could put thoughts that were difficult to say aloud.

He never expected anyone else to read it.

Unfortunately, one day something happened that changed everything.

One of Diana's friends was moving around Kevin's desk when she accidentally landed on his notebook.

She noticed the notebook and became curious.

Instead of leaving it alone, she opened it.

What she found inside revealed everything Kevin had written about his experiences with Diana.

By that time, their story was no longer a secret among the students.

Almost everyone in Senior One that year at Kisubi High School knew that Kevin and Diana had become close.

The notebook therefore became more than just Kevin's private thoughts.

The girl took the notebook to the staff and showed it to the class teacher.

At first, the teachers found it difficult to believe everything they had heard.

Kevin and Diana were called to explain what had happened.

When they were asked about their relationship, they finally admitted the truth.

They told the teachers that they had indeed been together.

That honesty helped them explain the situation.

However, the teachers made their decision clear.

Kevin and Diana were told that they should no longer speak to each other.

They both agreed.

From that moment, they tried to move forward with their lives.

But moving forward did not mean forgetting.

For Kevin, the memories remained.

He remembered the Thursday evening when he first saw Diana.

He remembered their first conversation.

He remembered the basketball pitch and the days when they could talk without feeling uncomfortable.

Most of all, he remembered the dance evening and the words that had changed everything.

Life at school continued, but it was never quite the same for Kevin and Diana.

Eventually, Diana changed schools.

And just like that, the story that had started with one unexpected moment came to an end.

Kevin remained at Kisubi High School, carrying with him memories that would become part of his life story.

Perhaps some stories are not meant to last forever.

Perhaps some people enter our lives for only a short time, leaving behind lessons that stay with us much longer.

Kevin and Diana's story began with an ordinary moment. But sometimes, an ordinary moment can become a memory that lasts for years.
