Something Was Changing

KISUBI HIGH SCHOOL • THE DAYS THAT FOLLOWED
Some friendships begin quietly, before we realize how important they are becoming.

After that first meeting, life at Kisubi High School continued as usual. Lessons continued, students moved around the school compound, and every day brought something new.

But for Kevin, something had quietly changed.

He had started noticing Diana more than he noticed before. Whenever he saw her around school, he would remember their first meeting and the unexpected moment that had brought them together.

Diana also seemed to notice Kevin whenever they crossed paths. Sometimes they would simply smile at each other. Other times, they would stop and talk for a few minutes before continuing with whatever they were doing.

Their conversations were simple. They talked about school, friends, classes and the ordinary things that filled their days.

Yet those ordinary conversations slowly became some of Kevin's favorite moments.

Morgan and Iman began noticing how often Kevin and Diana were around each other.

They would sometimes tease Kevin about it, but Kevin usually tried to act as though nothing unusual was happening.

Deep down, however, he knew that Diana was becoming someone special in his life.

Days passed, and the friendship between Kevin and Diana continued to grow.

The basketball pitch became one of the places where they often met. They would spend time there talking and laughing with their friends.

There was no pressure and no plan. They were simply enjoying each other's company.

Kevin began to look forward to seeing Diana every day. Sometimes he would find himself wondering whether she felt the same way about him.

But he kept those thoughts to himself.

Diana was also becoming more comfortable around Kevin. The shyness that had surrounded their first meeting slowly began to disappear whenever they were together.

They were no longer complete strangers.

They had become close friends.

Still, Kevin could feel that his feelings were beginning to change.

What he felt for Diana was becoming deeper than the friendship he had originally expected.

He didn't know whether he should tell her.

He was afraid that saying too much could change the friendship they had built.

So Kevin kept quiet and allowed the days to continue.

Then came the announcement that everyone had been waiting for.

There was going to be a dance evening at school.

The news quickly spread among the students, and the school became filled with excitement.

For Kevin, however, the dance meant something more.

He began wondering whether the evening would finally give him the courage to tell Diana what he really felt.

As the day of the dance approached, Kevin became more thoughtful.

He knew that sooner or later, he would have to face the feelings he had been keeping inside.

What had started as a simple accident on a Thursday evening had grown into something neither Kevin nor Diana could completely ignore.

And now, the dance evening was waiting.

Neither of them knew that the conversation that would happen that night would change everything.
