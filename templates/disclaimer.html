BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Author's Note

Welcome to LOVE AT FIRST SIGHT.

Before you begin reading, I want you to know something about the person behind these pages.

I am just a 14-year-old writer with a love for stories, creativity, and the courage to put my imagination into words.

This book is not written by someone who knows everything about life. It is simply a story created by a young person who wanted to tell a story and share a lesson along the way.

So please don't judge the book as if it was written by a professional novelist. Read it for the story, the emotions, the imagination, and most importantly, the message behind it.

"Every great writer begins as someone who simply decided to write."

Some parts of this story are fictional, and some ideas are inspired by the experiences and observations of growing up. The characters and events are created for storytelling purposes.

Most importantly, this book is not here to tell young people to rush into relationships. Growing up takes time, and everything has its season.

Read the story. Feel the journey. Take the lesson.

Written by MORGAN FRAMES
