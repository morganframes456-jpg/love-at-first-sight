Chapter One

The First Encounter

✦ ───── ♡ ───── ✦
KISUBI HIGH SCHOOL • THURSDAY EVENING
Sometimes the smallest moments become the memories we carry for the rest of our lives.

It all started with three friends who had just joined Senior One at Kisubi High School. Their names were Kevin, Morgan and Iman.

They were still getting used to their new school, their new surroundings and the unfamiliar faces surrounding them every day. Everything felt new, exciting and a little unpredictable.

It was a golden-hour Thursday evening. The sun was slowly beginning to disappear, leaving the school compound covered in a warm, beautiful glow.

Kevin, Morgan and Iman were moving around the school, enjoying the evening and talking about different things. Eventually, they decided to go toward Senior One West.

When they arrived, they found four girls together. Their names were Diana, Hailey, Husnah and Mercy.

The three friends decided to greet the girls and try to make some new friends. It seemed like an ordinary school evening, and nobody could have imagined what was about to happen.

They began talking and introducing themselves. Everything was going normally until Diana suddenly realized that she needed to leave.

She quickly turned around and started moving away.

Then, in one unexpected moment, Diana accidentally bumped into Kevin.

For a moment, everything seemed to slow down.

Kevin had seen moments like that in movies before. The kind of moment where two people unexpectedly meet and everything suddenly feels different.

But this wasn't a movie.

It was real life.

Diana looked at him and quickly apologized.

“Sorry... hey.”

Kevin answered quietly.

“Hello...”

There was a short silence between them.

Diana then introduced herself.

“I'm Diana. And you?”

Kevin smiled and answered.

“Call me Kevin.”

Both of them became shy. Neither seemed to know exactly what to say next.

Diana finally smiled and said,

“Nice to meet you, Kevin.”

Kevin replied,

“Me too.”

It was only a few words, but somehow the moment stayed in Kevin's mind.

To Kevin, that evening became one of the most memorable evenings of his life.

That night in the dormitory, while the other students were settling down, Kevin found himself thinking about Diana.

He kept remembering the moment they had bumped into each other, the way she had introduced herself and the short conversation they had shared.

He couldn't understand why such a small meeting had remained so strongly in his thoughts.

But something about that moment felt different.

Morning eventually came.

Kevin and Morgan were on their way to class when they found Diana along the way.

Kevin immediately recognized her.

Diana recognized him too.

They spoke to each other again, both becoming shy as they stood there talking.

It wasn't a very long conversation, but it was enough to make Kevin's morning feel different.

The next day, they met again.

And then they kept meeting.

Slowly, their meetings became a normal part of their school days.

Before long, Kevin and Diana started meeting around the basketball pitch. They talked, laughed and slowly became more comfortable around each other.

What had started with one accidental bump had slowly turned into a friendship.

Neither Kevin nor Diana knew what the future would bring.

They were simply two students who had met by chance and had begun becoming part of each other's everyday lives.

Sometimes, the biggest stories begin with the smallest moments.
