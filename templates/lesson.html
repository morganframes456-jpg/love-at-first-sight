Everything Has Its Time

✦ ───── ♡ ───── ✦
A MESSAGE TO THE YOUNG • MORGAN FRAMES
"You don't have to rush into relationships to prove that you are growing up. Some things are worth waiting for."

Relationships are not bad. Caring about someone, respecting them, and building a meaningful connection are beautiful parts of life. But everything has its time.

When you are young, there is still so much to discover about yourself. Your education, your dreams, your character, your talents and your future deserve your attention too.

Do not allow pressure from friends, social media or other people to make you believe that you must be in a relationship just because someone else is.

There is no prize for growing up too fast.

Build yourself first. Learn. Dream. Make mistakes. Discover who you are becoming. The right things in life do not always need to be rushed.

"Your future is bigger than a moment. Protect it."

If you are young and you have feelings for someone, remember that having feelings does not mean you must immediately turn them into a relationship. Respect yourself, respect the other person, and give yourself time to grow.

One day, you will look back and understand that some things were worth waiting for.
