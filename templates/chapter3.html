Chapter Three

The Dance Evening

✦ ───── ♡ ───── ✦
KISUBI HIGH SCHOOL • DANCE EVENING
Sometimes the words we fear most are the ones that change everything.

The day of the dance evening finally arrived. There was a different kind of excitement around Kisubi High School that day.

Students were talking about the event, laughing with their friends and looking forward to spending an evening away from the usual school routine.

But for Kevin, the dance was about more than music and excitement.

He had been thinking about Diana for days. The feelings he had tried to ignore were becoming harder to keep inside.

Kevin knew he needed to understand how Diana felt about him.

As the evening continued, Kevin eventually found the courage to call Diana aside.

His heart was beating faster than usual. He knew that whatever happened next could change the friendship they had built.

For a moment, neither of them said anything.

Kevin finally asked Diana whether she liked him.

Diana looked at him and answered honestly.

She told Kevin that she liked him as a friend.

Kevin listened quietly.

Her answer wasn't what he had secretly hoped to hear, but he respected her honesty.

Still, Kevin knew that keeping his own feelings hidden would only make things harder.

So he decided to tell her the truth.

Kevin explained that he liked Diana too, but not only as a friend.

Diana became quiet.

For a few seconds, the noise of the dance seemed distant as they stood there trying to understand what had just been said.

Then Diana told Kevin that she felt the same way.

Kevin couldn't believe what he had just heard.

It became one of the happiest moments he had experienced at school.

What had started with an accidental meeting had somehow grown into something neither of them had expected.

For Kevin, the evening felt almost unreal. He had spent so much time wondering what Diana thought about him, and now he finally knew.

But neither Kevin nor Diana understood what would happen after that conversation.

They had crossed a line from friendship into something they were not completely prepared to understand.

And that was when things slowly began to change.

After the dance evening, Kevin and Diana became unusually shy around each other.

The conversations that had once felt natural suddenly became difficult.

Sometimes they would see each other and simply look away.

They both remembered what they had said at the dance, but neither seemed to know what to say next.

It wasn't that the feelings had disappeared. Instead, both of them seemed unsure about what those feelings meant.

Days passed, and the distance between them slowly grew.

Eventually, Diana wrote Kevin a letter asking him whether he was angry with her.

Kevin replied that he wasn't angry with her.

He simply didn't know what to say or how to explain everything that was happening inside him.

Even after the letter, things did not return to the way they had been before.

The friendship that had once felt so easy was now surrounded by silence and uncertainty.

Kevin still remembered the first evening he had met Diana.

He remembered the accidental bump, the short conversation, the smiles and all the moments that followed.

He didn't know it yet, but those memories would soon become some of the most important memories he carried from his time at Kisubi High School.
